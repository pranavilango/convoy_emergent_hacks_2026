# Neighborhood Growth Map

create a landing page for a hackathon project, here is the description of the project:

one sentence pitch:
A preconfigured linear-time logistics analysis platform for SMEs to optimize scaling

problem:
Imagine you are a local bakery trying to delivery cupcakes to more customers. You have limited resources, so how you spend them is crucial. Currently, the softwares you could use require technical knowledge, are expensive and don't tell you actionable steps to take.

solution:
Convoy is a growth planner built specifically for small businesses. Upload your current network: locations, working hours, order sizes, and vehicles, and Convoy will tell you what practical changes will yield the highest impact: e.g.: where to open a new store, what neighbourhood to expand your delivery service to, optimal working hours, etc.
